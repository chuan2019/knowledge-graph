About the dataset

This is a synthetic media-operations knowledge graph modelling the content delivery supply chain of a fictitious media company. All data is generated for demonstration purposes. The graph contains roughly 5,000 titles, 15,000 versions, 200 clients, 50 regions, 30 languages, 150 delivery points, 50,000 rights grants, 10,000 localization jobs, 900 delivery specs, and 20,000 delivery requests.

Catalog

  • Title — films and TV series with genre, studio, and release year.
  • Version — format variants of a title: resolution (HD/4K/8K), HDR format, audio channels, localization flag.

Rights & Clients

  • Client — studios, broadcasters, and streamers tiered as Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3.
  • Rights — territorial grants with start/end dates, exclusivity windows, and active status.
  • Region — 50 countries grouped by continent.

Localization

  • Language — 30 languages with family grouping.
  • LocalizationJob — dubbing or subtitling tasks with completion status, vendor, and quality score (0–100).

Delivery Pipeline

  • DeliveryPoint — named endpoints (e.g. streaming platforms, broadcast outlets) linked to a region.
  • DeliverySpec — format requirements per delivery point and version.
  • DeliveryRequest — orders with priority, deadline, and status (Pending / In Progress / Completed / Failed / Delayed).
